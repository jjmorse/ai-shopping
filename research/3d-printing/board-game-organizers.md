## Need

Removable organizers that speed setup and fit the editions actually owned. Ordinary PLA and the stock 0.4 mm nozzle are sufficient for the projects below; neither clear filament nor TPU is a prerequisite. Verify sleeves, exact box, bed fit, and hardware before printing a complete set.

## Wingspan magnets

The earlier V1 research called for 60 magnets, 6 mm diameter × 3 mm thick, for the full organizer. V2's discussed count was 56 and should not be substituted. Recheck the selected V1 files/instructions before ordering or gluing; a partial set can need fewer magnets.

Josh proposed a [Rhinocats Amazon pack](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F8BGRJ5L/) with the nominal required dimensions. Its current stock, price, dimensional accuracy, and purchase completion were not established. An earlier Amazon suggestion became unavailable; do not repeat availability claims without checking.

Print a magnet-pocket fit check and confirm polarity before securing magnets. For the other models, no magnet/screw/glue requirement was identified in the earlier pages; that is not a guarantee that every file variant has none. Waterdeep explicitly adds the rubber-band storage recommendation.

## Initial print workflow

1. Choose the files for the exact edition and owned expansions. For Wingspan, account for possible tight sleeves before printing card wells; for paid models, resolve the noted compatibility questions before purchase.
2. Open the designer's project in Bambu Studio, select the actual X2D/nozzle/material, and check each object's size against the bed. Use each required plate once unless the instructions call for duplicates.
3. Start with a small tray, card fit-check, or magnet-pocket sample. For the Wingspan starter, our suggested baseline was PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% gyroid, supports off, and 100% scale; these were our starting settings, not a designer-prescribed profile.
4. Inspect the slice preview, first layer, and critical fits before printing the full organizer. Material and time estimates above come from earlier profiles and can change with Studio settings.
5. Keep game-specific hardware and sleeve notes with the chosen files. No organizer download or slicing was performed by the assistant during this research import.
