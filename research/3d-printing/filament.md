## Notes

- AMS: use plastic spools or reprint-a-spool adapters; cardboard spools shed dust and can slip. Refills need a Bambu reusable spool.
- Silk PLA is more brittle and moisture-sensitive; dry before use and print slower for best shine. Pick the "Generic PLA Silk" profile for non-Bambu spools.
- Clear/transparent PLA prints frosted, not glass-clear. Use it for lamp shades and diffusers. Getting it clearer takes 100% infill, a hot nozzle and post-processing; PETG gets clearer.
- AliExpress: prices seen signed out can differ from Josh's cart (new-shopper prices, per-store shipping). Check the final total. Platform coupons ($X off $Y) usually work across stores, once per order.
