## What the phone supports

- **No Qi2, no magnets, not even "Qi2 Ready."** OnePlus said magnets cost too much weight/space next to the 7,300 mAh battery ([Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/phones/oneplus/does-the-oneplus-15-support-qi2-magnetic-charging), [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/phones/oneplus-phones/magnets-are-too-heavy-oneplus-exec-explains-why-the-oneplus-15-doesnt-have-pixel-style-magnetic-charging)).
- **50W AIRVOOC** (OPPO/OnePlus proprietary) with a OnePlus wireless charger.
- **Standard Qi works but is slow:** HowToGeek's review measured only ~5W from a Qi2 charger ([review](https://www.howtogeek.com/oneplus-15-review/)). So any generic Qi/Qi2/MagSafe pad, stand, or car mount is a trickle charger on this phone.
- OnePlus left the US in 2026; oneplus.com/us no longer sells accessories. Buy from authorized resellers (Swiftronics, Amazon "OnePlus Store") or eBay imports.

## Recommendation

**Buy:** OnePlus AIRVOOC 50W Magnetic Charger, $59.99 at Swiftronics, plus the official Sandstone magnetic case ($19.99) if Josh doesn't already have a magnetic case. ~$80 total before shipping/tax.
**Runner-up:** AIRVOOC Magnetic Charger 2 + stand, $89.99 on eBay, if he wants a built-in stand and can wait until mid/late December.
**Why:** It's the only route to fast wireless on this phone; everything Qi-only is ~10x slower. The v1 puck is $30 cheaper, US-stocked, and was tested by Tom's Guide on the OnePlus 13 (with OnePlus Sandstone case): 0–100% in 70 min, steady 36W after warm-up ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/oneplus-phones/i-tried-oneplus-new-wireless-charger-on-the-oneplus-13-and-iphone-16-pro-max-heres-the-results)). Expect longer on the 15's bigger battery.

**By use:**
- Nightstand/desk: puck above. Sits on its side as a lean-against stand; Tom's Guide found face-down on the fan cut out after 28 min, so don't lay the phone flat on it.
- Car: no US AIRVOOC car mount found. Qi2 mounts work for holding with a magnetic case but charge ~5W; a wired SUPERVOOC car charger is the fast option.
- Travel: same puck + brick.
