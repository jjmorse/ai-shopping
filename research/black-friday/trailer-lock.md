## Need

Strong, hard-to-defeat coupler lock. Cheap universal/tri-ball locks (CURT, Reese, Master) are easy to cut or pry.

## Options summary

Proven Industries is the standard high-security brand: custom-fit stainless body, shrouded puck lock, made in USA. Models fit specific coupler types; ~$250 each, no Black Friday sale seen. Trimax UMAX100 is a mid-tier universal option.

## Recommendation

**Buy:** Proven Industries coupler lock matched to your coupler (2516 for trigger/thumb latch, 2178 for most A-frame/straight, 2517-D1 for Demco cast, 2516-B2 for cast steel).  
**Runner-up:** Trimax UMAX100.  
**Why:** Fitted shell leaves no gap to pry or cut; best-known deterrent.

## Notes

Add a wheel boot for layered security when parked long-term. Need coupler brand/style to choose model — check [Proven's product guide](https://www.provenlocks.com/search?q=coupler+lock).
