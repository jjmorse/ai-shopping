# Trailer lock

Data: [`lists/black-friday-2026.yaml`](../../lists/black-friday-2026.yaml) → item `trailer-lock`
Checked: 2026-11-26

## Need

Strong coupler lock for 2022 Escape 21NE. Escape uses a 2" ball and a generic Atwood-style yoke-latch coupler ([Escape forum](https://www.escapeforum.org/forums/f10/trailer-hitch-coupler-9290.html)). Cheap tri-ball locks are easy to defeat.

## Options summary

- Blaylock TL-33: US-made, heavy steel, fits most A-frame couplers incl. Atwood 2" per etrailer Q&A. Price unconfirmed.
- Proven 2178-A (~$250): Escape owners' usual pick, fitted shell. Ethics concern below.
- Layer: wheel boot or a heavy cable/chain lock through a wheel/axle — owners note thieves can bypass a coupler lock by chaining up.

## Recommendation

**Buy:** Blaylock TL-33 + a wheel lock for storage.  
**Runner-up:** Proven 2178-A, if you're OK with the company.  
**Why:** Blaylock is solid without the Proven baggage; layering does more than any single coupler lock.

## Notes

Proven Industries controversy (2025): YouTuber Trevor McNally opened Proven's $130 latch pin lock with a shim cut from a soda can. Proven sued him (copyright, defamation, etc., M.D. Fla. 8:25-cv-01119); the judge denied its injunction June 2025 and Proven voluntarily dismissed in July 2025. Owner Ron Lee sent McNally a "be prepared!" DM and texted his wife. Sources: [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/lock-company-tried-suing-a-guy-for-picking-its-lock-it-backfired), [AllAboutLawyer](https://allaboutlawyer.com/proven-locks-lawsuit-dismissed-lock-company-voluntarily-drops-case-after-youtuber-exposes-130-lock-flaw-with-soda-can/). The shim worked on their pin lock, not shown on their coupler locks.
