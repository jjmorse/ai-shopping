## Need

Get the most back on Black Friday / Cyber Monday buys using Josh's cards (Amazon Prime Visa, Target Circle Card,

Microsoft Cashback), plus alternatives where they clearly pay more.

## Big picture

1. **Rotating 5% categories don't help this year.** Chase Freedom Q4 = grocery, dining, Red Cross
   (activate by **Dec 14**). Discover Q4 = entertainment, restaurants, utilities, and **streaming is excluded**.
   Neither covers Amazon, Target, Walmart or general retail.
2. **So the card is simple:** Amazon → Prime Visa 5%. Target → Target Circle Card 5%. Costco → Costco Citi 2%.
   **Everything else → Fidelity 2%.** Freedom/Discover only for groceries/dining/utilities.
3. **The portal is where the money is.** Pick the single best portal per store (only the last click counts),
   and don't use a coupon code the portal doesn't list, since that usually voids the cash back.
4. **Store promos and discounted gift cards beat portals on the big items** (PetSmart points, Google Play Home Depot gift cards,
   free Best Buy Plus).

## Recommendation

**Buy:** Litter-Robot at **PetSmart today or tomorrow** (offer ends 11/29) through Rakuten at 15%, with the Fidelity card.
That's the single biggest win (~$290 back on $725). For everything else, use the table: Fidelity 2% plus the best single portal.

## Shop through Chase

Only earns when paying with the Freedom (Ultimate Rewards points). CashbackMonitor lists it at up to 6x Best Buy and up to 8x Home Depot (category-based).
Freedom earns 1x base, while Fidelity earns 2%, so Shop through Chase wins only when its rate beats the other portals by more than 1 point.
Needs a Chase login to see rates; I didn't sign in.

## Before you check out

- Click through the portal **last**, in one tab, with other cashback extensions (Capital One, Honey, Rakuten/Microsoft) not also activating. The last click wins.
- Don't paste coupon codes the portal doesn't list (Rakuten: "will void Cash Back" at Litter-Robot and PetSmart).
- Check the card apps for **Chase Offers, Discover deals, and Citi Merchant Offers** (card-linked, stack with portals). These need your login.
- Elevated Rakuten rates ("was 2%") usually reset after Cyber Monday. End dates aren't shown signed out.
